About RingSizeFind

Welcome to RingSizeFind! Buying jewelry online should be exciting, but all too often, it comes with a major headache: figuring out your exact ring size. Between confusing international conversion charts and inaccurate home measurement tricks, it is incredibly easy to accidentally order the wrong size.

We built this website to solve that exact problem. RingSizeFind is a dedicated, programmatic sizing utility designed to convert physical millimeter dimensions into standardized US, UK, and European ring sizes with absolute mathematical precision.

Meet the Creator

Hi, I am Dhiraj Gurung, the founder and creator of RingSizeFind. Like many online shoppers, I struggled with the inconsistency of digital ring sizing charts and vague sizing instructions across different storefronts.

Combining my background in digital development with an interest in accessible e-commerce utilities, I launched this platform to give everyday buyers and jewelry makers a reliable, unbloated tool they can trust before making a purchase decision.

Our Sizing Methodology

We don't guess when it comes to dimensions. Our interactive calculator and digital sizing algorithms are fully calibrated against the official ISO 8653 jewelry manufacturing standards. This international baseline ensures that the millimeters you measure at home seamlessly map onto global retail sizing, whether you are shopping from the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Australia, or Europe.

Our Commitment to Accuracy

While we use modern coding and software assistance to keep our calculations lightning-fast, every guide, tip, and troubleshooting method hosted on this site is manually written, physically tested, and verified by me. We routinely test our printable home charts against physical standard steel mandates to make sure your home tests are as accurate as possible.

We are entirely independent and focused strictly on utility. If you ever have questions, feedback on our calculator, or want to suggest a new sizing feature, please don't hesitate to reach out via our Contact Page.
